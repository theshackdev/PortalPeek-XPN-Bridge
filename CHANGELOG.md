# Changelog

## 1.1.0
- First release. Portal Peek records every portal you stand by, so one-way links (XPortalNetworks) show where they lead.
- Day and night views: each portal keeps a daytime and a nighttime recording and shows the one for the light you're in; until both exist it falls back to the other one (or a recording made before the bridge).
- Client-side; nothing is sent or saved besides Portal Peek's own recordings.
